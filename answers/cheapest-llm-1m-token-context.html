What you can actually fit in 1M tokens

A million tokens is roughly 750,000 words — about seven average novels, a mid-sized codebase, or six months of a busy team’s chat logs. If your workflow involves whole-document analysis, long transcripts, or big code contexts, that window removes the chunking-and-stitching problem entirely.

When a premium long-context model is worth it

The cheap end of the table is fast, light models tuned for retrieval-style work. If your task is dense multi-document reasoning — comparing 40 contracts, debugging across an entire repo — quality still scales with model size. The premium end of the 1M-context market:
